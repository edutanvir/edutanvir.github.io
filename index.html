About my work

Engineering data for better decisions.

Most data starts out messy: files in odd formats, columns that don't match, numbers nobody quite trusts. I like the part of the job where that mess turns into something a person can actually use to make a decision.

Pipelines I can trust

I move data from where it starts to where it's useful, with checks along the way so problems show up early instead of in a meeting.

Architecture you can follow

I map the route the data takes, from raw files to reports, and explain why each layer exists, so the next person never has to guess.

Answers, not just charts

A dashboard should answer a question. I write up what it shows, what it can't tell you, and where the numbers came from.
